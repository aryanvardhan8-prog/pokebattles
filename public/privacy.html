Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

DailyBattles is a free, non-commercial fan game run by one person. This page explains what information the game keeps, why, and what you can do about it. In short: we keep only what the game needs to work. We don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

1. What we collect

If you play as a guest (no account):

  • Game saves on your device: your battles, streaks, stats, saved teams and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are only sent to us if you post a result or create an account.
  • A random device ID and time played: to count how many people play and for how long, the game creates a random ID on your device and sends the total time the game is open. It isn't linked to your name, email or anything that identifies you.

If you create an account, we also keep:

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our login provider, and we can never see it.
  • Your username, country (to show a flag) and chosen trainer picture.
  • Your game results (daily battles, Elite Four runs, the teams you used and the moves you chose, so results can be checked), your stats, saved teams and friends list.
  • Friend battles: the matches you play, the teams you pick and each turn's choices.
  • Phone notifications, only if you turn them on: a technical address that lets us send notifications to your device, plus which kinds you want.

Feedback: if you send feedback, we keep your message, the kind (idea, bug or other), your username if you're logged in, the page you were on and your device type, plus your email only if you choose to give it.

2. What other players can see

Your username, country flag, trainer picture, results and the teams in your results are shown on the leaderboards and to your friends. Your email is never shown to anyone.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game: log you in, save your progress, show leaderboards, run friend battles and send the notifications you asked for.
  • To keep the game fair: battles are replayed on our server to check results are real.
  • To improve the game: read feedback and see overall numbers (such as how many people played today).

We never use your information for advertising, and we never sell it.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts accounts, the leaderboard and saved data.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and receives feedback forms.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Apple or Google): delivers notifications if you turn them on.

These services handle data only to provide their service, under their own privacy policies. Their servers may be outside your country.

5. Cookies and local storage

DailyBattles doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and it doesn't use third-party analytics. It uses your browser's local storage (similar to cookies) to keep you logged in and to save your game, settings and the random device ID described above. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. This deletes your guest progress on that device.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. Leaderboard results are kept as part of the game's history. If you ask us to delete your account, we'll delete your account details and remove or anonymise your results.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Play as a guest without giving any personal information.
  • Change your username, country and trainer, and turn notifications on or off, in your profile.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account, using the feedback form in the game (choose "Other" and include your username).

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or India), you may have further rights under local law. We'll honour any reasonable request.

8. Children

DailyBattles is for a general audience. You need to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in your country) to create an account. If you're younger, play as a guest. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Connections to the game are encrypted (HTTPS), and passwords are never stored in readable form. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is big, we'll also mention it in the game.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: use the feedback form at the bottom of the game's main menu.
